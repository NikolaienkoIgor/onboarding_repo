# Generative Engine Optimization

## How Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Works

### What is GEO?

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is the process of making a company and its expertise easier for AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity to understand and mention.

**SEO helps people find your website.**

**GEO helps AI understand your company.**

---

### How does GEO work?

Imagine someone asks ChatGPT:

> "How to fix e-invoice XML?"

AI looks for information about this topic, compares different sources, and uses the information it considers useful and trustworthy to create an answer.

Our goal is to make sure that:

**E-invoice Repair → Fintom8**

is clearly explained across our website and other relevant sources.

---

### What do we need to do?

GEO is mainly about creating **clear and consistent information**.

We should explain:

- What Fintom8 does
- What problems it solves
- What our technical terms mean
- How our technology works
- Which standards we work with
- How our products relate to these standards

For example:

> **Pre-ERP Invoice Repair** is the automated detection and correction of invoice errors before an invoice enters an ERP system.

The more clearly we explain a concept, the easier it is for AI to understand it.

---

### Where should we publish?

#### 1. Our website

The website should be the **main source of information**.

For example:

- What is Pre-ERP Invoice Intelligence?
- What is Pre-ERP Invoice Repair?
- EN 16931 Explained
- Peppol Invoice Validation
- Syntax vs. Semantic Errors
- ZUGFeRD
- ERP Invoice Processing

These pages should be connected and explain how the topics relate to each other.

#### 2. External sources

We can also share useful technical information on:

- GitHub
- SAP Community
- Developer forums
- Industry websites
- Technical publications
- Partner websites

The goal is not to copy the same article everywhere.

The goal is to have **different reliable sources supporting the same knowledge about Fintom8**.

---

### How long does GEO take?

GEO is **not an instant process**.

AI systems need time to discover information, understand it, and compare it with other sources.

There is no guaranteed date when a company will appear in AI answers.

The most important things are:

**Quality → Consistency → Useful Content → Reliable Sources → Regular Updates**

It is better to create **10 strong, connected pages** than 50 short articles with little useful information.

---

### GEO vs. SEO

| Area | SEO | GEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main goal | Helps people find a website | Helps AI understand a company |
| Focus | Search rankings | AI-generated answers |
| Key signal | Keywords | Concepts and context |
| Trust signal | Backlinks | Citations and reliable sources |

---
