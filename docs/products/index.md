# Products

Fintom8 products for pre-ERP invoice intelligence: validate, repair, extract, and convert EN 16931 / Peppol / ZUGFeRD documents.

| Product | What it does |
|---------|----------------|
| **[Corrector](corrector.md)** | Fixes invalid Peppol BIS / UBL (and related EN 16931) XML with a model + validation checks (cloud baseline: Gemini 3.5 Flash ~30 s) |
| **[Dell hardware](dell-hardware.md)** | On-site Corrector servers: S = R360/R660 CPU, M = R760 + L4, L = L40S or two L4s |
| **[eFTI](eFTI.md)** | Explains eFTI, national e-transport systems, and how freight data relates to e-invoicing |

More product pages will be added here as they are documented for onboarding.
