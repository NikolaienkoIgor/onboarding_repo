# E-Invoice News

We use these news portals to stay up to date on how things are going with e-invoicing.

- [VATCalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/blog/): Tracks worldwide tax reforms, ViDA (VAT in the Digital Age) updates, government tax mandates, and digital reporting rules across various countries.
- [The Invoicing Hub](https://www.theinvoicinghub.com/): An informational platform offering news, country-by-country profiles, and educational resources covering mandatory B2B/B2G e-invoicing standards, such as Peppol and EN 16931.
- [Shared Services Link](https://sharedserviceslink.com): Provides industry news, best-practice webinars, white papers, and expert analysis on enterprise financial automation, SAP upgrades, AP/AR processes, and regulatory compliance strategies.
