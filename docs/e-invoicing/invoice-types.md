# Invoice Types

There are three common types of invoices in business processes:

## Ausgangsrechnung — Outgoing Invoice

An outgoing invoice is an invoice that a company **sends to its customer** for goods or services. It records money that the company expects to receive.

## Eingangsrechnung — Incoming Invoice

An incoming invoice is an invoice that a company **receives from a supplier** for goods or services. It records money that the company needs to pay.

## Gutschrift — Credit Note

A credit note is used to **reduce or correct the amount of an existing invoice**. For example, it can be issued for a returned product, an overcharge, or a cancelled service.
