# Market Actors

The e-invoicing ecosystem is shaped by different market actors — from ERP and accounting platforms to network organizations and industry associations. Each plays a different role in how invoices are created, processed, validated, exchanged, and regulated.

### SAP

[SAP](https://www.sap.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a major ERP platform used by enterprises to manage finance, procurement, and business processes. Through **SAP Document and Reporting Compliance**, SAP supports the creation, processing, validation, and exchange of electronic invoices. Its integration with Peppol enables companies to send and receive e-invoices through the Peppol network, while SAP maps transactional data into the required electronic invoice format and performs validation checks.

### DATEV

[DATEV](https://www.datev.de/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a major German provider of accounting and tax software used by businesses and tax advisory firms. DATEV supports the digital processing of incoming and outgoing e-invoices, including structured formats such as **ZUGFeRD and XRechnung**. Its E-Rechnungsplattform can also connect invoice flows with Peppol and other networks, allowing electronic invoices to move directly into DATEV accounting workflows.

### Lexware

[Lexware](https://www.lexware.de/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) provides accounting and financial software primarily for small and medium-sized businesses in Germany. **Lexware Office** supports creating, receiving, processing, and archiving e-invoices in formats such as **ZUGFeRD and XRechnung**. The platform validates incoming e-invoices for technical validity and integrates their processing into the accounting workflow.

### OpenPeppol

[OpenPeppol](https://peppol.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is the international non-profit organization responsible for governing and maintaining the **Peppol interoperability framework**. Peppol standardizes how organizations exchange electronic business documents through common specifications such as **Peppol BIS Billing**, while its four-corner model allows buyers and suppliers to exchange documents through accredited service providers. Importantly, **Peppol is the framework and network ecosystem — not an invoice software provider itself**.

### GENA

[GENA — Global Exchange Network Association](https://www.gena.net/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a global, non-profit industry association bringing together companies involved in **e-invoicing, compliance, procurement, ERP, finance, and digital networks**. Unlike SAP, DATEV, or Lexware, GENA does not function as an ERP or accounting platform; it acts as an industry community that supports interoperability, regulatory dialogue, collaboration, and the broader adoption of digital business transactions.
