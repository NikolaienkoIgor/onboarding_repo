# Market Actors

The e-invoicing ecosystem is shaped by different market actors — from ERP and accounting platforms to network organizations and industry associations. Each plays a different role in how invoices are created, processed, validated, exchanged, and regulated.

### SAP

[SAP](https://www.sap.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a leading enterprise software provider offering ERP, e-invoicing, compliance, and Peppol integration. It targets large organizations and provides highly automated, integrated invoice processing.

### DATEV

[DATEV](https://www.datev.de/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a German accounting and tax software provider offering e-invoicing, accounting, and digital document processing. It mainly serves businesses and tax advisors with integrated and automated financial workflows.

### Lexware

[Lexware](https://www.lexware.de/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) provides simple accounting and invoicing software for small and medium-sized businesses. Users create invoices through the software, while Lexware handles the required e-invoice formats such as ZUGFeRD and XRechnung.

### OpenPeppol

[OpenPeppol](https://peppol.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) governs the Peppol framework and defines how organizations exchange standardized electronic documents. It provides the rules and network ecosystem rather than accounting or ERP software.

### GENA

[GENA — Global Exchange Network Association](https://www.gena.net/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is an industry association connecting companies in e-invoicing, ERP, finance, procurement, and digital networks. Its role is to support interoperability, cooperation, and the development of the global e-invoicing ecosystem.

### CEN

CEN develops European standards that ensure different systems and markets can work together. In e-invoicing, CEN provides the standardization framework behind EN 16931.

### CEN/TC 434

**CEN/TC 434** is the technical committee responsible for European e-invoicing standardization. It developed **EN 16931**, which defines the core semantic model for electronic invoices and helps ensure that invoice data can be understood across different systems.
