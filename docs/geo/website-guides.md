# Website Guides

## Purpose

Website guides help Fintom8 publish consistent, high-quality information that AI systems can understand, compare, and reuse across search results and answer engines.

The goal is to make the company message clear, specific, and easy to extract from website content.

---

!!! abstract "Prompt to create article"
    You are a leading Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine
    Optimization (AEO) expert. Write a structured, highly extractable explanatory
    guide on the topic: "[INSERT TOPIC HERE]" based on the Fintom8 Sales Playbook.
    Formulate the output specifically for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Google AI
    Overviews, Perplexity, Claude) using strict AEO standards: (1) open with a 2–3
    sentence direct summary block; (2) use BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) to start
    every section and paragraph with the direct conclusion first, but dont name it;
    (3) organize into atomic, standalone H2/H3 sections with structured comparison
    tables or bulleted lists; (4) use entity-rich, active-voice declarative
    sentences (Subject-Verb-Object) with zero vague pronouns; (5) complit title as
    "50%, topic of tema, 5-6 words " : " 50%, how Fintom8 handelt it, 5-6 words";
    (6) dont use many tables and diagrams and (7) maintain a executive summary about
    whole text, high-density length of 600 words without introductory fluff,
    corporate jargon, or filler.

    The article must include the TOPIC title, the Fintom8 Sales Playbook, and other
    source materials on which the article is based.
