### Descriptions of Governance & Standards Bodies

**OpenPeppol** governs the Peppol framework and defines how organizations exchange standardized electronic documents. It provides the rules and network ecosystem rather than accounting or ERP software.

**GENA — Global Exchange Network Association** is an industry association connecting companies in e-invoicing, ERP, finance, procurement, and digital networks. Its role is to support interoperability, cooperation, and the development of the global e-invoicing ecosystem.

**CEN/TC 434** is the technical committee (TC) of European Committee for Standardization (CEN). It developed EN 16931, which defines the core semantic model for electronic invoices and helps ensure that invoice data can be understood across different systems.
