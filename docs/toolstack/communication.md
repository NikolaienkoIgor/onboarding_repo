# Communication

Clear, async-first communication is a core part of how we work at Fintom8. We default to **written, searchable communication** so that knowledge is accessible to everyone, regardless of time zone.

---

## Slack

Slack is our primary messaging platform for day-to-day communication.

### Getting started

1. Accept the invite to `fintom8.slack.com`.
2. Fill in your profile: **full name**, **role**, **team**, and a profile photo.
3. Enable **2FA** on your Slack account.

### Key channels

| Channel | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| `#general` | Company-wide announcements |
| `#engineering` | Engineering discussions and news |
| `#onboarding` | Questions from new joiners — ask anything here! |
| `#introductions` | Introduce yourself to the company |
| `#releases` | Automated deployment notifications |
| `#alerts` | Production monitoring alerts |
| `#it-helpdesk` | IT access requests and support |
| `#random` | Off-topic conversation and fun |

### Slack etiquette

- **Prefer public channels** over DMs for work topics — it keeps knowledge accessible.
- Use **threads** to keep discussions organised within a channel.
- Set your **status** when you're out of office, in deep focus, or on leave.
- Respect **working hours** — avoid expecting instant replies outside 9–18 CET.
- Use `/remind` to schedule follow-ups so nothing falls through the cracks.

!!! tip "Do Not Disturb"
    Configure your notification schedule in Preferences → Notifications so Slack doesn't ping you outside working hours.

## Microsoft Teams

Teams is used primarily for **cross-team meetings, video calls, and communication with non-engineering stakeholders**.

- All-hands and town-hall meetings are held in Teams.
- Use Teams for calls with external partners or clients.
- Meeting recordings are stored in Teams channels automatically.

---

## Email

Email (`@fintom8.com`) is for **formal and external communication**:

- Contracts, vendor communication, compliance notices.
- Calendar invites for recurring meetings.
- Anything that needs a permanent, formal record.

Use Slack or Teams for everything internal and day-to-day.

---

## Video calls — norms

| Norm | Why |
|------|-----|
| Camera on by default | Builds team rapport |
| Mute when not speaking | Reduces background noise |
| Use a virtual background if needed | Maintains professionalism |
| Share an agenda before the call | Keeps meetings focused |
| Write a summary / action items after the call | Creates a searchable record |

---

## 1Password (Password Manager)

We use **1Password Teams** to share credentials securely.

- Never share passwords over Slack, email, or any plaintext medium.
- Store service credentials, API keys, and shared accounts in the relevant 1Password vault.
- Use the browser extension for auto-fill.
- Request vault access from **#it-helpdesk**.
