# Chapter 1: Toolstack

Welcome to your first chapter. Understanding our toolstack is one of the most important things you can do in your first week — it will help you contribute quickly and communicate clearly with your team.

---

## Overview

At Fintom8 we follow a **cloud-native, DevOps-first** philosophy. Every tool we choose is evaluated on three criteria:

1. **Collaboration** — does it make working together easier?
2. **Automation** — does it reduce repetitive manual work?
3. **Observability** — does it help us understand what our systems are doing?

## Quick-start checklist

Complete these steps in your first three days:

- [ ] Install VS Code and the recommended extensions (see [IDE & Editors](ide.md))
- [ ] Clone your first repository and make a test commit (see [Version Control](version-control.md))
- [ ] Say hi in **#introductions** on Slack (see [Communication](communication.md))
- [ ] Open your first Jira ticket (see [Project Management](project-management.md))
- [ ] Review a sample GitHub Actions pipeline (see [CI/CD](cicd.md))

!!! warning "Access issues?"
    If any tool listed here is inaccessible, ping **#it-helpdesk** on Slack straight away so we can get you unblocked.
